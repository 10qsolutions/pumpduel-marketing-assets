## Before posting

1. Open each advertised code and confirm the **correct exercise and timeframe**, not merely an HTTP success or generic page.
2. Confirm a live round is available, and check its dates, rules, entry and attempt terms.
3. Test the link and code through the homepage and the supported installed iPhone app, including a fresh entry.
4. Recheck at round rollover. Current entry links should reach the new round; old result links must retain their original round.
5. Review the original PNG on a phone, then add native links and the correct caption in the social app.

Do not invent winners, scores, prizes, free access, dates or participant consent. The initial pack contains no real participant data. No social posts are sent automatically.
